# RGC (Really Good Culture) — product intel (scraped via Chrome, 3 Oct 2026)

**Positioning:** "The world's first predictive retail intelligence system. Predict how likely a product, brand or trend is to succeed in any retail environment." Product = **Breakthrough**. Sister product = **Watch Humans** (video reviews → behavioural insight; every review mapped to 400+ Shopper Missions; £999 retail-insight add-on; Oddbox case study).

**Customers:** Retailers (category directors, buyers, range planners), Major brands (commercial directors, sales), Challenger brands (founders: "compelling data that influences buyers"), Own-brand/NPD teams.

**Seven dimensions:** 1 Product Graph & Retail (every GTIN/EAN, shelf, range, price/promo, store-format nuance), 2 Brand Performance, 3 Culture & Trend, 4 Market & Macro, 5 Behavioural, 6 Biometric, 7 Synthetic.
Scale claims: 4M+ products, 56,000+ attributes, 28 behavioural-science principles.

**Use cases listed:** Whitespace analysis, Winning listings, JBP evidence, Marketing plans, Private label NPD, Range effectiveness, High-ROAS content, Supplier negotiations, Query agent.

## Blog theses (their worldview — judges will nod at these)
- *Winning listings without a sales history* (Adam, co-founder): circular problem — "you need the listing to generate the data, and you need the data to win the listing." Buyers discount DTC/social traction because it doesn't predict *their* shelf. Buyer actually needs: where volume comes from, recruits new shoppers vs cannibalises, incremental category value.
- *Why range reviews reward the past* (Sophie, Product Lead — a judge/organiser): review answers "what performed last year?" and substitutes it for "what will perform for the next 18 months?". Lead times lengthening, categories moving faster.
- *The problem with the best data in retail* (David, co-founder): EPOS shows a single historical scenario; "says nothing about the ranges you did not run, the prices you did not test, or the shoppers who walked past without buying."
- *How synthetic shoppers fill the evidence gap* (Ege, AI engineer): synthetic shoppers grounded in Watch Humans observed behaviour + 28 principles; "only as credible as the behavioural data underneath them, which is why the grounding matters more than the simulation."
- Also: Whitespace is a forecast, not a gap (Sean); The end of gut-feel category decisions (Adam).

## Implication for our hack
- Don't rebuild Breakthrough. Build a **new signal / grounding layer** they lack, or attack a gap their own blog names:
  1. "Shoppers who walked past without buying" — rejection / near-choice data (unobserved in EPOS).
  2. Grounding validity of synthetic shoppers — calibrate personas against real revealed behaviour (Ege's point).
  3. Cold-start challenger evidence (Adam) — volume sourcing / cannibalisation forecast with no sales history.
  4. Store-format/environment transfer (brief track 2).
  5. AI-agent shoppers as a new "shopper archetype" that EPOS/Watch Humans can't see.
