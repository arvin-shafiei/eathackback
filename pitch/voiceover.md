# simsbury: voiceover (2:00)

Read this out loud while you record. The cue in brackets says what to click; don't read the brackets.

**[Store opens. A shopper picks something up and puts it back.]**

Every shop owner asks one question: why didn't that sell?

The till can't answer. It never saw the person who picked it up, looked at it, and put it back.

**[Zoom out over the whole store.]**

So we built a shop that can see it.

This is simsbury. Real UK products, and shoppers built from ten thousand real Reddit comments and shelf research. Every choice is made by TypeSafe Jev, and written down.

**[Click a shopper.]**

Click anyone. Here's what they're thinking, and what's in their trolley.

They put this back. Why? Here's the reason, how sure we are, and the real Reddit post it came from. Not a guess. A reason.

**[Open analytics. Best and worst sellers at the top.]**

Now do that for three hundred shoppers.

Your ten best sellers. Your ten worst. One click shows where you lose people.

Small challenger brands get picked up as often as the big names: thirty-five percent against thirty-four. But they're kept far less: forty-eight percent, against fifty-four for big brands and sixty-seven for own-label.

They don't lose on the shelf. They lose in the hand. And now you know.

**[Open your store. Heatmap on. Slide "hide quiet areas".]**

If you own the shop: red is where customers crowd, blue is where they hardly look. That blue is shelf space that isn't working.

**[Open rearrange. Pick the top category.]**

So don't just know it. Fix it.

Pick a category and it tells you why: this product sells when people see it, but it's stuck on the bottom shelf.

**[Show the numbered arrows. Press "watch it", then "do it".]**

Here are the moves, in order. Watch it. Then test it on new shoppers before you move a single tin.

**[Open shoppers. Use a basket. Show the next-visit route.]**

And your regulars get a short route to their usual things, plus one new thing they'll like.

**[End card: simsbury.]**

Brands. Store owners. Shoppers.

Stop guessing why it didn't sell.

simsbury. Now you know.
