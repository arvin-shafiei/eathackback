# simsbury: voiceover (2:00)

Read this out loud while you record. The cue in brackets says what to click; don't read the brackets.

---

**[Store opens. A shopper picks something up and puts it back.]**

Every night, a shop owner looks at the till and asks one question: why didn't that sell?

The till can't answer. It only knows what left the building. It never saw the person who picked it up, looked at it, and put it back.

**[Zoom out over the whole store.]**

So we built a shop that can see it.

This is simsbury. A living supermarket with real UK products, and shoppers built from ten thousand real Reddit comments and published shelf research. Every choice they make is decided by TypeSafe Jev, and every one is written down.

**[Click a shopper.]**

Click on anyone. Here's what they're thinking. Here's what's in their trolley.

They picked this up and put it back. Why? Here's the reason, here's how sure we are, and here's the real person on Reddit who said it first.

That's the difference. Not a guess. A reason, and where it came from.

**[Open analytics. Best and worst sellers at the top.]**

Now do that for three hundred shoppers.

Your ten best sellers. Your ten worst. One click and you see exactly where you're losing people.

And here's what the till could never show you. Small challenger brands get picked up just as often as the big names: thirty-five percent against thirty-four. But they're kept far less. Forty-eight percent, against fifty-four for the big brands and sixty-seven for own-label.

They don't lose on the shelf. They lose in the hand. And now you know.

**[Open your store. Heatmap on. Slide "hide quiet areas".]**

If you own the shop, here's where your customers actually go. Red is where they crowd. Blue is where they hardly look. That blue is shelf space that isn't working for you.

**[Open rearrange. Pick the top category.]**

So don't just know it. Fix it.

Pick a category, and it tells you why, in plain English. This product sells when people see it, but it's stuck on the bottom shelf.

**[Show the numbered arrows. Press "watch it", then "do it".]**

Here are the moves, in order. Watch it happen. And before you move a single tin, test it on brand-new shoppers.

**[Open shoppers. Use a basket. Show the next-visit route.]**

And your regular customers? After a re-layout, they get a short route straight to their usual things, plus one new thing they'll actually like.

**[End card: simsbury.]**

Brands. Store owners. Shoppers.

Stop guessing why it didn't sell.

simsbury. Now you know.
